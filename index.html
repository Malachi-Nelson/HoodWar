    Port Calder Open World

    No rounds and no clock. Six crews split a city that carries on around them, each with one neighbourhood and its eye on the middle of town. Take any car or helicopter, call your crew, rob the bank, buy up the block, take turf street by street and live off what it pays, and stay ahead of the police, or join them. Online, ride together as one crew or pick rival sides.

      Touch: left thumb on the stick moves (push all the way to sprint), drag anywhere on the right to look. FIRE shoots, and dragging it aims while it fires. ADS aims down the sights (tap on, tap off, or hold: your pick below). USE gets in cars, opens shops and robs tills; tap a prompt to do the same. In a car the stick steers and drives. In a helicopter the stick flies it, UP and DOWN set your height, FIRE works the nose guns and ROCKETS fires from the pods. MAP and TURF open the map and the turf board; PHONE calls your homies, the mechanic, the fixer or someone with work. Move buttons also changes what any button does, hides the ones you never use and adds buttons of your own. A controller works too.

      Progress is kept in this browser.

      Keyboard and mouse, or touch on a phone or tablet. Esc pauses. The ` key opens the dev menu. Cash, guns, rank, businesses, your car and your turf are saved as you play, and your turf and businesses keep earning while you are away. A player's headshot kills in one shot. Multiplayer: one player opens a lobby, the others join and ready up, and the leader starts the session; anyone who arrives later joins it where it stands. Everyone plays on the leader's crew, and players cannot hurt each other. Multiplayer runs through a free public relay: everyone on the plain link shares one public room, and Make a private room gives you a link for just your group.
