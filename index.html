      Keyboard and mouse, or touch on a phone or tablet. Esc pauses. The ` key opens the dev menu. Cash, guns and your turf are saved as you play, and your turf keeps earning while you are away. Multiplayer: one player opens a lobby, the others join and ready up, and the leader starts the session; anyone who arrives later joins it where it stands. Everyone plays on the leader's crew, and players cannot hurt each other. Multiplayer runs through a free public relay: everyone on the plain link shares one public room, and Make a private room gives you a link for just your group.
